🌡️ What is El Niño?

El Niño is a climate phenomenon associated with unusual warming of ocean temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean.

El Niño can influence weather patterns in many parts of the world. In some regions it can contribute to reduced rainfall, drought conditions, higher temperatures or unusual rainfall.

Farmers can be affected because changes in rainfall and temperature can influence water availability, planting schedules, crop growth and pest pressure.

🌍 Global Warming & Agriculture

🌡️

Higher Temperature

Increasing temperatures can create heat stress for crops and livestock.

🌧️

Changing Rainfall

Rainfall patterns may become less predictable, creating risks for farming.

💧

Water Shortage

Drought and reduced water availability can affect irrigation and crop production.

🐛

Pest Risk

Temperature and weather changes can influence pest and disease conditions.

🌾 Main Problems Faced by Farmers

🌧️ Irregular Rainfall

Farmers may experience delayed rainfall, insufficient rainfall or unexpected heavy rain.

🔥 Extreme Heat

High temperatures can cause heat stress and reduce crop productivity.

💧 Lack of Water

Drought conditions can reduce water available for irrigation.

🌱 Crop Damage

Weather extremes can damage crops during important growth stages.

🐛 Pests & Diseases

Changing weather conditions can increase some pest and disease risks.

💰 Financial Loss

Crop losses can reduce farmer income and increase production costs.

💡 Solutions & Prevention

💧 Water Management

Use efficient irrigation methods and conserve available water.

🌱 Climate-Smart Crops

Consider locally recommended crop varieties suited to expected conditions.

🌾 Crop Diversification

Growing different crops can help reduce dependence on a single crop.

🌦️ Weather Monitoring

Check reliable weather information before important farming operations.

🐛 Field Monitoring

Regularly inspect crops for pests and diseases and follow local agricultural advice.

📱 Digital Technology

Use weather and agricultural information systems to support farming decisions.
